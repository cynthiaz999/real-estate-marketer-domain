Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Real Estate Copy Generator (the “Service”) is operated by property-marketer.com (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Service, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.
  • Usage information. We keep a count of how many generations you have used, tied to your email address, so we can apply free-trial and monthly plan limits. If you subscribe, we also keep your Stripe customer ID so we can link your account to your subscription.
  • Subscription and payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details directly. We never see or store your full card number. We receive your subscription status and plan from Stripe so we know which limits apply to you.
  • Content you submit. The property notes you paste into the Service are sent to our AI provider to generate your marketing copy. We do not store your notes or the generated copy in our database. OpenAI may keep the notes you submit and the copy it generates for up to 30 days for abuse and safety monitoring, and under its API data policy does not use them to train its models. Please do not include personal information about third parties (such as a seller’s phone number or a home’s occupants) in your notes unless you need it for the listing.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and identify your account
  • To generate the marketing copy you request
  • To apply free-trial and plan limits and manage your subscription
  • To respond to support requests
  • To protect the Service from abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and emails

Cookies. When you sign in with Google, the Service sets a cookie so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe may set its own cookies on its payment pages.

Emails. We do not send marketing emails. Stripe may email you receipts and billing notices, such as a failed-payment notice, about your subscription. We may also email you in reply to a support request.

Who we share information with

We use the following service providers to run the Service. Each receives only the information needed to do its job and handles it under its own privacy policy.

  • Google, for sign-in
  • Stripe, for payments and subscription management
  • OpenAI, to generate copy from the notes you submit
  • Supabase, to store your email address, usage counts, and Stripe customer ID
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run), which provides the application framework and hosting. Hosting providers keep routine technical logs (for example, error logs).

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Billing records are kept by Stripe according to its own policies and applicable law. When you ask us to delete your information, we will delete your email address, usage counts, and Stripe customer ID from our database within 30 days; records that Stripe or the law requires to be kept are not covered by that deletion.
